This policy explains what information GTA Clipper ("we", "the app") accesses and how it is used.

Information we access

  • Connected publishing accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages) owned by the GTA Clipper team: basic profile information (such as the account ID and display name) and the permission to upload videos.
  • Access tokens issued by those platforms through their official login (OAuth).
  • Public live-stream content (video, audio and public chat) from streamers who have agreed to be clipped.

How we use it

  • Tokens are used only to upload clips to the accounts that authorized the app, and to check the status of those uploads.
  • Stream content is processed to create short highlight clips that credit the original creator.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens are stored on our own server and are never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Raw stream recordings are deleted automatically after a few hours.
  • We do not collect information from viewers of the published videos.

Third-party services

Uploads use the TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API and Facebook Graph API, each governed by its own privacy policy. Use of YouTube is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the security or connected-apps settings of TikTok, Google or Facebook. On request to the contact email below, we delete all stored tokens and data for an account within 30 days. Streamers can ask us to stop clipping their content and to remove published clips at any time.
