GTA Clipper is a private tool used by its owner to publish short vertical highlight clips (under 60 seconds) from Grand Theft Auto live streams on Twitch and Kick, with the permission of each streamer.

How it works

  1. The tool watches the public live streams of streamers who have agreed to be clipped.
  2. It selects the best moments, converts them to vertical format and adds subtitles and visible credit to the original creator.
  3. It uploads the clips to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels accounts owned and operated by the GTA Clipper team, using each platform's official API.

Who can use it

Only the owner of GTA Clipper connects accounts to this tool. It is not offered to the public and does not access any third-party user's account.
